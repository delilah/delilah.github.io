WHAT I DO

With a tech background, I often find myself programming, but I'm just as involved in the creative process. I enjoy wholesome projects with a purpose. And spooky things.

I build experiences and help shaping them - be it games, products, interactive software or installations, AR/VR, taking it from scratch to final polishing.

I collaborate closely with designers, teams and stakeholders to translate ideas into testable prototypes fast, and bridge tech and design.

Some of my tasks are:

  • Gameplay & interaction systems (mostly Unity/C#)
  • Experiment with several technologies for the perfect match
  • Hardware/CV integrations for installations
  • Team leadership, onboarding, mentoring, and process
  • Collaborate in the creative process: brainstorming, ideation, storytelling
  • Prototyping sprints to de‑risk concepts
  • Implementing and designing core, game mechanics and/or interactive features
  • Implement design / UI / audio / tooling
  • Work with third-party APIs
  • Help define project requirements and estimate timelines

  • A task I never did? It's ok, give me some time, I'll figure it out.

I like GIT, I use tickets and I can roll spaghetti very fast.

I do also value and keep learning about communication, leadership, diversity and how to best support people around me. I advocate for inclusivity and embracing a wide range of perspectives and backgrounds.

I empower my teams by fostering open collaboration, leading by example, ensuring everyone’s voice is heard, and giving credit where it’s due.

Clear communication and transparency are key. I bring my experience in remote workflows to ensure the team stays aligned, helping set up efficient pipelines and communication practices that keep everyone on the same page, whether we’re in the same room or across the globe.
