Tic Tac Chew & Play

Chew‑controlled Snapchat game. FWA of the Day.

Video

Description

First chew-controlled Snapchat game. Face-tracking mechanics using Snap Lens, mapping chewing to gameplay input.

A custom game engine has been built on top of the existing Lens Studio engine as no gaming feature (game loop, effects, score, etc.) existed at the time of the development.

  • Awards: FWA of the Day (22 Jun 2018)
  • Tech: Lens Studio, Typescript, Custom Game Engine
  • Platform: Snapchat mobile app
  • Studio: Monks
