Description

Development of AR Group Effects for Messenger video calls using the Spark AR Multipeer API. These interactive multiplayer AR experiences transform video calling by enabling shared, real-time augmented reality interactions between multiple participants simultaneously.

Group Effects allow users to play multiplayer games, interact with shared AR objects, and experience synchronized visual effects during video calls. The project involved creating dynamic, real-time interactive effects that work across multiple devices and participants in Messenger video calls and Messenger Rooms.

  • Tech: Spark AR, Multipeer API, JavaScript, TypeScript
  • Platform: Messenger (iOS/Android/Web)
  • Features: Real-time multiplayer AR, synchronized effects, interactive games
  • Studio: Monks, META
