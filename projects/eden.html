Netflix: Eden Unearthed (VR)

Explore Netflix's Eden universe in VR. This immersive experience brings the animated series to life through interactive environments and engaging mechanics that complement the show's narrative themes.

Video

Description

Explore Netflix's Eden universe in VR through "Eden Unearthed", a free VR game/experience for the Oculus Quest 2 based on the anime series Eden.
Gameplay: Ride a bike in a post-human future to collect apples for biofuel, learn about the world from robots, and uncover mysteries related to the anime's story.

Available on the Oculus App Lab.

  • Awards: FWA of the Day (12 Nov 2021)
  • Tech: Unity, Oculus SDK
  • Platform: VR headsets (Oculus Quest and Oculus Quest 2)
  • Studio: Monks
